Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 May 2026

1. What we collect

When you sign in to Adcreo using Google OAuth, we collect your Google account email address, name, and a unique Google ID. We do not collect passwords.

When you create a video ad, we store the product image you upload, the generated scripts, and the output video file. These are stored securely on Cloudflare R2 storage.

2. How we use your data

Your data is used solely to provide the Adcreo service — generating video ads from your product images. We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising purposes.

3. AI-generated content

All video ads generated by Adcreo are AI-created. Each video is signed with a C2PA (Content Authenticity) certificate, which embeds a tamper-evident record of AI involvement as required by the IT Rules 2026.

4. Data retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. Generated video files are retained for 90 days after creation. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

5. Third-party services

Adcreo uses the following third-party services: Google OAuth (authentication), Cloudflare R2 (file storage), and Neon Postgres (database). Each service has its own privacy policy.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights or to delete your account, email us at hello@adcreo.in.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at hello@adcreo.in.
